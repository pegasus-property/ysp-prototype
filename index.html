Our mission

To bring together young professionals with ties to Slovakia, build meaningful connections across industries, and create opportunities to learn from and support one another.

There are not that many of us outside Slovakia, and most of us arrived without a community to turn to. YSP is the one we wished we had: a place to find each other, share what we know and open doors.

How YSP is run

  • A written constitution and a Leadership Council that decides by majority vote.
  • Elected officers: President, Vice-President and Treasurer. The Treasurer oversees the budget.
  • Every new initiative needs a vote of the full council.
  • Leaders rotate as they graduate, with a handover process so the work continues.
  • We work alongside Slovak PRO, the network of Slovak professionals in North America.

What supporters can expect

  • A yearly report: members, events, mentor matches, scholarships awarded and money spent.
  • One named leader as your contact.
  • Recognition on this site and at events, if you want it.
  • Member data stays private. The directory is members-only and never shared or sold.

Mentorship

Launching soon

Someone a few steps ahead, who gets where you're from.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you need or offer

    Your field, where you are in school or work, and what you want out of it.

  2. The mentorship team makes a match

    A person reads every request and pairs you by field, goals and location.

  3. You meet, we follow up

    You get an introduction by email. We check in later to see how it went.

For example: recruiting for banking internships, choosing between law schools, moving back to Slovakia.
